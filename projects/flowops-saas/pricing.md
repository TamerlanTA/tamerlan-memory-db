## Monthly Subscription Tiers

### Maintain — $149/month
- 1–2 active pipeline-системы
- Monthly health check
- Bug fixes & error monitoring
- Workflow updates (minor)
- Email support (48h response)
- Infrastructure hosting
- Telegram status alerts

### Scale — $299/month
- 3–5 active pipeline-систем
- Weekly health check
- Priority bug fixes
- Workflow improvements (up to 2/month)
- Email + Slack/Telegram support (24h response)
- Infrastructure + redundancy
- Monthly operations call (30 min)

### Operator — $549/month
- 6–10 active pipeline-систем
- Real-time monitoring
- Unlimited bug fixes
- 4 workflow improvements/month
- Priority support (same-day response)
- Dedicated ops manager
- Monthly strategy call (60 min)
- Custom reporting

### Enterprise — Custom
- 10+ pipeline-систем
- SLA договор
- Custom pricing
- Dedicated team

## Pricing Display Strategy (Site)

- **Entry и Standard pipelines**: показываем "From $X setup + from $Y/mo"
- **Complex pipelines**: "From $X setup" с note "custom quote for enterprise"
- **Subscription**: полностью открытые цены на /pricing
- **Bundles**: выделить как "Best Value"
- **НЕ показывать**: точные цены до квалификации для Tier 3–4

---

## Anti-Commodity Signals

1. Никогда не говорим "мы дешевле" — говорим "мы вернём X в месяц"
2. ROI calculator на каждой pipeline detail page
3. "Typical ROI within 60–90 days" на homepage
4. Case study format: "Problem → Pipeline → Result" (конкретные цифры)
5. Pricing framing: "The cost of one missed client covers the monthly subscription"
